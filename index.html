The Atelier

Chiaroscuro Meets Architectural Minimalism

Founded in 2024 by cinematographer-turned-photographer Julian Vance, Wave & Lens Studio approaches every commission as a still frame from an unmade European art film. We favor sculptural directional light, tactile silver-halide grain, and quiet emotional candor over transient trends.

Operating from our 3,200 sq. ft. north-facing daylight loft in Manhattan’s Cast-Iron District, our team travels worldwide for editorial campaigns, destination celebrations, and private portrait sittings.
